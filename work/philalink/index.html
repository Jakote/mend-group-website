Philalink

A booking platform for a mobile mechanic service, where the mechanic comes to the customer. It takes the booking, sends the nearest free mechanic, and lets the customer watch them arrive.

The problem

A mechanic who drives to the customer needs three things to line up: a booking with the car, the work and the price agreed; a mechanic who is free and close by; and a customer who knows when they will arrive. Philalink wanted customers, mechanics and the office working from one system.

What we built

  • Booking in four steps: vehicle, services, place and time, then a review with the total before the customer confirms.
  • Dispatch: a new booking goes to the nearest available mechanic within 50 km with nothing else booked at that time. If there is none, it stays open for a nearby mechanic to accept or the office to assign.
  • Live tracking: the mechanic shares their position during the job and the customer sees a map and an arrival time.
  • Notifications in the app and by email when a job is assigned or its status changes.
  • Inspection checklists with photos, and priced quotes the customer accepts from a link, with a PDF.
  • Separate signed-in areas for customers, mechanics and the office, each limited by database access rules.
