TM Stone Solutions

TM Stone makes wetcast concrete pavers and lays them. We built the company's website with a live quote calculator, and a back-office the owner runs the business from on his phone.

Client
TM Stone Solutions (PTY) LTD, Maseru
Built
August to October 2026, in five phases
Stack
HTML, CSS and JavaScript, no framework. Supabase (PostgreSQL with row-level security). GitHub Pages.
Status
Website in private preview. Back-office built and tested, not yet in daily use.

Open the website and try the calculator

The problem

A customer who wanted a price had to message or phone and wait. The owner priced each job from a cost sheet and ran quotes, jobs, stock and payments himself. Cement, sand and pigment prices move, so a quote sent last month can be wrong this month.

What we built

  • Phase 1: a website with the company's own project photos, and a calculator that prices supply from the published price list.
  • Phase 2: quotes and a costing engine that prices each paver from the batch recipe, supplier prices and monthly running costs.
  • Phase 3: jobs and production: what is promised, what is poured, crew dates, progress from site, and job cards.
  • Phase 4: stock and supplier orders, worked out from what was poured.
  • Phase 5: invoices, deposits, payments and what is still owed.
  • Then a sales list for enquiries, a monthly report and staff records.

What it does today

  • A customer types an area, picks a paver and sees the supply price at once. One tap sends it to TM Stone on WhatsApp with the paver, area and price filled in.
  • Book a delivery at a new price and the saved quotes still out show, in Maloti, what the change costs.
  • Two jobs on the same crew with overlapping dates are flagged.
  • It keeps working with no signal and catches up when the phone reconnects. Two phones see the same records.
  • 13 automated test suites run on every change to the back-office code, including database access rules and a two-phone sync test in a real browser.

Status

The website is in private preview and moves to tmstone.co.ls when the domain is registered. The back-office is built and tested. It goes into daily use once the owner's own database account is set up, so his data sits in his account, not ours.
