Mafisa, Pitso and Matsema

Three apps on one shared system. Mafisa lends equipment people are not using. Pitso finds a plumber, welder or tailor for one job. Matsema lets small producers pool supply to fill an order none could fill alone.

Owner
MEND GROUP (PTY) LTD

Stack
JavaScript installable phone apps, no framework. Database written for Supabase (PostgreSQL with row-level security); the pilot keeps data on the phone for now.
Status
Pilot on sample data. No users yet.

Open the pilots

Why one system

Each app needs the same four things: who a person is, which of their documents have been checked, what others say after working with them, and a record that cannot be quietly changed. Built once, a tradesperson's standing on Pitso is the same standing behind their bakkie on Mafisa.

What we built

  • Sesotho and English throughout, switched with one button.
  • Installable on a phone, and built to keep working without a connection.
  • Mafisa lists an asset only after a document proving ownership is checked, or says plainly that ownership is not proved.
  • An append-only record of agreements, with a button that checks nothing in it was changed.
  • It records what two people agreed and when both marked it settled. It never holds or moves money.

The apps

Live at platforms.mendgroup.co.za. Every person, business and buyer in them is invented, and each app says so on screen.

What it does today

  • All three run in a phone browser today, on sample data.
  • The database tests are written as things it must refuse: editing or deleting a recorded agreement, listing a bakkie or tractor without a checked ownership document in the owner's name, booking the same bakkie twice for the same days, and a review with no completed job behind it.

Status

A pilot with no users yet. This page will say so until that changes.
